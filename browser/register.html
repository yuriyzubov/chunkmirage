Register in the browser

Aligns a moving image onto a fixed one in two steps: an affine places it roughly (scale, rotation, shift), given or found here, then this computer's GPU fits a smooth field of local shifts on top, and, if asked, finer fields where you zoom in. The images are read straight from their URLs and nothing is uploaded; the registered volume is computed here, chunk by chunk, as the viewer asks.

Fixed image

The reference. It stays where it is.

Moving image

Warped onto the fixed image's grid.

Solver settings

Iterations and window take one value, one per level, or one per level with the refined ones below (fine levels gain from a wider window, such as 15 or 31).
